Today, on your 24th birthday, my heart is overflowing with so much gratitude and tenderness that words can barely capture it. The universe was exceptionally kind when it brought you into this world, because you were destined to become the sweetest blessing of my life.

Happy Birthday, my sweet Tulsi. May this 24th year bring you boundless happiness, peace, success, and every bit of magic that your beautiful soul deserves.

Forever & unconditionally yours,
